WHY ELDERSAFE

Instant Alerts

HOW IT WORKS

From detection to response.

ELDERSAFE connects the sensing device, patient, and emergency response team into one real-time system.

01

Detect

The connected device monitors movement and detects a potential fall.

02

Transmit

The event is securely transmitted to the ELDERSAFE backend.

03

Alert

The patient and emergency response dashboard receive the alert instantly.

04

Respond

The responding team can accept the emergency and navigate to the patient.
